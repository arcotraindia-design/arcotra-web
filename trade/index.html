Quarry to project

India to the world.

India is one of the largest producers of natural stone on earth, and its ceramic industry ships to every continent. We work at both ends of that: choosing the material, and getting it to your site.

Indian marble collection

Ten marbles, quarried in India.

Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat and Uttar Pradesh. Blocks vary — we send photographs of the actual lot before anything is cut for you.

Indian ceramic collection

Ten tile ranges, made in India.

Floor and wall tile across the finishes that actually move — from high-gloss vitrified to large-format porcelain.

We are on both sides of the material. We specify stone and tile in our own design projects and we supply it — so what we recommend comes from having drawn the room, not from what is sitting in a warehouse.

That is the part clients use most: we advise on what to specify — which material suits the use, which finish survives it, how much waste to allow for, and where the money is better spent elsewhere. If something you are asking for is wrong for the job, we will say so.
