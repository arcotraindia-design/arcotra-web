Construction  —  03

Renovation is design before it is demolition.

Old buildings hide things. The drawings are wrong, walls are not where they should be, and something structural is always where you wanted an opening. We design the change first, then carry it out — so surprises are answered on paper, not by three people standing in the dust.

How it works

The process, step by step.

01

Consultation

What you want the building to do that it currently does not.

02

Survey

Measured survey of what is actually there, not what the old plans claim.

03

Planning

What can move, what cannot, and in which order the work has to happen.

04

Cost estimate

Priced by stage, with a clear allowance for what old buildings hide.

05

Execution

Demolition, structure, services and finishes.

06

Quality control

Checks before each stage is covered over.

07

Handover

Completion and the record of what was actually done.
