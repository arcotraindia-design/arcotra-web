Construction  —  01

Houses built the way they were drawn.

Building a home is where most budgets slip and most timelines break. We take the drawings — ours or someone else’s — and turn them into a costed programme, then run the work against it.

How it works

The process, step by step.

01

Consultation

What is being built, where, and to whose drawings.

02

Site assessment

Access, ground conditions, services, and what the site will allow.

03

Planning

Sequence, trades, and a realistic programme rather than an optimistic one.

04

Cost estimate

A priced breakdown by stage, so you can see where the money goes.

05

Execution

The build itself, run against the programme.

06

Quality control

Checks at each stage, before the next one covers up the last.

07

Handover

Snagging, completion and the documents you will need later.
