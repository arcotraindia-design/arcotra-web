Architecture  —  04

Drawings other people can actually read.

If you already have the design — your own, or from another architect — we produce the images that make it understandable. Developers use them to sell, architects to present, families to decide. The work is the same: get the light, the materials and the framing right.

How it works

The process, step by step.

01

Brief

What the images are for, and who will look at them. A sales render and a decision render are not the same picture.

02

Drawings & references

Plans, elevations or a model, plus reference images for the mood you want.

03

Materials

We agree the finishes before modelling, because changing them later costs time.

04

Camera views

We propose the views and you confirm them from a rough draft, not from a finished image.

05

Draft render

Low-resolution passes for composition and light. Changes are cheap here.

06

Final delivery

Full-resolution images, plus one round of minor revisions.

What we need from you

  • Architectural drawings or a 3D model
  • Accurate dimensions
  • Reference images
  • Material and finish list
  • Which views you want
  • How many views
  • Output resolution
  • Revision expectations
