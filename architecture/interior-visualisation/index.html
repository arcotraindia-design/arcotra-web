Architecture  —  04

Showing a space that does not exist yet.

Sometimes the design is already settled and what is missing is the picture. A manufacturer needs their product shown in the shop it is sold for. A developer needs an apartment shown furnished. A designer needs their own scheme rendered properly. We build the space in 3D and light it so it reads as a photograph.

How it works

The process, step by step.

01

Brief

What the images are for and who decides from them. A brochure image and a client approval image are framed differently.

02

What you send

Drawings, a sketch, product dimensions, or photographs of the real space. We work from whatever exists.

03

Materials & setting

Finishes, lighting and the kind of room it belongs in. A shelving unit in a bakery is not the same picture as the same unit in a pharmacy.

04

Layout & camera

We block out the scene and agree the views from a rough draft, before anything is lit.

05

Lighting & dressing

The stage that decides whether it looks real. Light direction, reflections, and the objects that make a room look lived in.

06

Delivery

Full-resolution images plus one round of minor revisions.

Specification

What we can supply.

—

Retail and product

A shelving manufacturer’s range shown stocked and lit in the shop it is sold for. An empty steel frame tells a buyer nothing; a full aisle tells them everything.

—

Residential interiors

Apartments and houses shown furnished, so a buyer can picture living there before the plaster is dry.

—

Material options

The same room rendered with two worktops or three floor finishes, so the decision is made on screen instead of after the joinery is built.

—

Output

Still images at print or web resolution, in the aspect ratio you need for catalogues, listings or presentations.

What we need from you

  • Product or room dimensions
  • Drawings or photographs
  • Material and finish details
  • The setting it belongs in
  • Reference images you like
  • Number of views
  • Output size and format
  • Whether text or branding goes on the image
