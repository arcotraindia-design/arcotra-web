Architecture  —  02

The face a building shows the world.

Sometimes the plan is fixed — the house exists, or the layout is already decided — and what needs work is the outside. Facade composition, materials, openings, roof line, and how the building sits in its plot.

How it works

The process, step by step.

01

Discovery

What is already built or decided, and what you want the building to say.

02

Survey & drawings

Existing elevations, photographs from every side, and the surrounding context.

03

Concept

Material palette and facade composition. Two directions, one chosen.

04

Design

Detailed elevations with materials, finishes and dimensions.

05

3D visualisation

Daylight and evening views, so you see the building as it will actually be seen.

06

Final proposal

Drawings and a material schedule ready for costing.
