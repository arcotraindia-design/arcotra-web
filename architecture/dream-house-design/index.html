Architecture  —  01

A house designed from the ground up.

Most people come to us with a plot, a rough idea and a budget. We turn that into a buildable design: floor plans that work for how your family actually lives, elevations you can picture, and 3D views that let you walk through the house before a foundation is poured.

How it works

The process, step by step.

01

Discovery

We start with the plot and the people. How many of you, how you live, what you liked and disliked about every home you have had.

02

Site & requirements

Plot dimensions, orientation, access, local building rules, and the practical constraints that shape what is possible.

03

Concept

Two or three directions for the plan and the massing. We agree on one before any detail is drawn.

04

Design development

Floor plans, elevations, sections. Room by room, with real dimensions and real materials.

05

3D visualisation

Exterior and interior views. This is where most decisions get made, because you can finally see it.

06

Final proposal

Drawing set, material schedule and a package your builder can price and work from.

What helps us start

The more of this you have, the faster the first conversation goes. None of it is required to begin.

  • Plot location and dimensions
  • Number of floors
  • Bedrooms and bathrooms
  • Who will live there
  • Architectural style you like
  • Approximate budget
  • Desired timeline
  • Any local building restrictions
